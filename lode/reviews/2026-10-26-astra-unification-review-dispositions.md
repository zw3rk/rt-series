| **1** | **Critical** | The original "5-insn Soft-TLB" used `ubfx x2, x1, #8, #12` (bits 8–19 instead of `(gva>>12)&0xfff`), invalid register-offset `ldp x2, x3, [x20, x2]` (rejected by assembler), clobbered `NZCV` via `cmp`, and did not check alignment, read vs write permissions, or cross-page boundaries. | **RESOLVED** | Replaced in **§4.3** with an assembler-verified (`clang -c` + `objdump -d`) **7-instruction AArch64 Inline Soft-TLB** using 32-byte `rt64_tlb_entry_t` (`read_tag` at `+0`, `addend` at `+8`, `write_tag` at `+16`): `ubfx x2, x1, #12, #12` -> `add x2, x20, x2, lsl #5` -> `ldp x2, x3, [x2]` (or `[x2, #8]` for stores) -> `and x4, x1, #~(0xfff - (N-1))` -> `sub x4, x4, x2` -> `cbnz x4, .L_tlb_miss_stub` -> `ldr/str`. Checks VPN tag, read/write permission, natural alignment (`(gva & (N-1)) == 0`), and non-page-crossing simultaneously without modifying `NZCV`. |
| **2** | **Critical** | Class-A Darwin (`vm32.c`) cannot enforce 4 KiB permissions using VMA bookkeeping alone while executing direct `[x20, wAddr, uxtw]` loads/stores on 16 KiB Darwin pages. | **RESOLVED** | Specified **Most-Restrictive Host Page Projection + Sub-Page Fault Mediation** in **§4.4**: each 16 KiB host page is `mprotect`ed to the intersection (`sub_prot[0] & sub_prot[1] & sub_prot[2] & sub_prot[3]`) of its four 4 KiB guest sub-pages. Uniform 16 KiB regions run at full `[x20, wAddr, uxtw]` hardware speed; accesses into mixed-permission 16 KiB pages trap into `signals.c`, which either delivers `SIGSEGV` (if disallowed by `sub_prot[q]`) or single-steps the allowed access via `interp_step`. |
