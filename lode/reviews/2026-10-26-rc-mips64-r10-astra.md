**Detailed findings**

**Medium — Child death before acknowledgment permanently hangs the descriptor-exhaustion fallback.** At [vm_file.c:159](/Users/angerman/Projects/zw3rk/mips64rt/src/vm/vm_file.c:159), the parent waits solely for `fork_ack`. If `pipe()` fails with `EMFILE`/`ENFILE`, fork succeeds, and the child is killed before [publishing acknowledgment](/Users/angerman/Projects/zw3rk/mips64rt/src/vm/vm_file.c:175), that value remains zero forever.

The surviving parent spins while retaining both locks, blocking its fork and other family inode operations. The pipe path handles this case through EOF. This also affects processes without guest file mappings because the constructor installs the handlers unconditionally.

The fallback needs a handshake that detects child death. Simply timing out and unlocking would reopen r9’s race. This finding is source-derived; it was not reproduced during this review.
