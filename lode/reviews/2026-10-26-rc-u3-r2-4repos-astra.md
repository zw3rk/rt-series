New runtime calls require unsuffixed `rt_host_map_file()` and `rt_host_protect()`, but these are defined only in the Linux backend, which Darwin builds exclude. See [vm32.c:97](/Users/angerman/Projects/zw3rk/sparc32rt-rcu3-sparc32/src/vm/vm32.c:97), [vm_file.c:102](/Users/angerman/Projects/zw3rk/sparc32rt-rcu3-sparc32/src/vm/vm_file.c:102), [vm_mmap.c:90](/Users/angerman/Projects/zw3rk/sparc32rt-rcu3-sparc32/src/rt32/vm_mmap.c:90), [vm_mmap.c:154](/Users/angerman/Projects/zw3rk/sparc32rt-rcu3-sparc32/src/rt32/vm_mmap.c:154), and [Makefile:41](/Users/angerman/Projects/zw3rk/sparc32rt-rcu3-sparc32/Makefile:41).
