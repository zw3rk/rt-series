## 1. Cross-Model Verification Summary

Both `astra#xhigh` (`gpt-6-astra` at `xhigh`) and `fable#high` (`claude-fable-5-1` at `high`) independently verified the Rev 3 AArch64 Soft-TLB fast-path sequences:
- `astra#xhigh` verified **688,128 `(width, page, tag, offset)` cases** in Python and confirmed all four `and` logical-immediate masks encode in A64, `ldp x3, x2, [x2, #8]` is legal non-writeback A64, `PSTATE.NZCV` is preserved across instructions 1–6, and `cbnz` ±1 MiB reach holds with per-block cold stubs.
- `fable#high` assembled every sequence in §4.3 with `clang -arch arm64` (including `N = 16` mask `0xfffffffffffff00f`), verified register tables against all seven `trampoline_a64.S` files, and confirmed all cited line numbers across `hyper-linux` and the 10 translator repos.

More importantly, `astra#xhigh` (15 findings: 4 Critical, 10 Major, 1 Minor) and `fable#high` (23 findings: 4 Critical, 10 Major, 9 Minor) **independently converged on the same four root architectural flaws in Rev 3**, which neither `gpt-6.1-sol#medium` pass had caught:
1. **Class-A Dual-View `G + S` (`MAP_SHARED` memfd/shm) on `H > 4096` hosts breaks `fork()`, breaks `MAP_PRIVATE` file mappings, causes RELRO + `.data` `SIGSEGV` trap storms, and triggers double-side-effect / non-atomic `interp_step` replay bugs** (`astra#xhigh` #1, #2, #3, #4; `fable` `C1`, `C4`, `M3`, `M9`).
2. **Relocating a `MAP_PRIVATE` page (`H1 -> H2`) during COW promotion breaks `CLONE_VM` thread coherence (`read_tag` keeps `H1`), breaks `futex` wait/wake address matching, and breaks contiguous `guest_base + gva` spans** (`fable` `C2`, `M2`; `astra#xhigh` #1, #9, #10).
3. **Stop-the-world quiescence deadlocks when a worker spins in chained DBT blocks (`while (!ready) ;`), when two mutators contend on `g_vm_mutation_lock` before parking, or when host `ftruncate`/`mprotect` fails after Step 3 already mutated VMA state** (`astra#xhigh` #5, #6; `fable` `C3`, `M4`).
4. **Provider A and Provider B (`hyper-linux`) require cross-`fork` inode coordination, physical GPA allocation on `PTE_COW_CLEAN -> PTE_COW_DIRTY`, active vCPU preemption (`hv_vcpus_exit`), translator DBT block invalidation, pre-bucket-lock futex pinning, and per-class Darwin `kqueue`/`wake_pipe` blocking** (`astra#xhigh` #7–#14; `fable` `M5`–`M10`).
